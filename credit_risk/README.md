This project is a credit risk modeling exercise, done on publicly available Lending Club data from kaggle.
The objective is to demonstrate an start to end risk modeling workflow (PD estimation, expected loss, economic capital, and reject inference),
and not a production-ready model.

Several simplifying assumptions were made such as:
Static logistic regression, instead of survival modeling
Simplified LGD and EAD assumptions (e.g lgd = 0.6)
Limited reject inference due to feature mismatch
No macroeconomic stress testing

So, numerical outputs should be interpreted as examples, not of any sort investment advices.
Future improvements will be made.
